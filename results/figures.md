Generated by `scripts/make_figures.py` from the measured results. PNG and PDF versions are in `results/`.

## Figure 1 — percent of roofline by engine

`results/fig1_roofline_by_engine.png` / `.pdf`

Bars are at 128 tokens; whiskers show the min–max across 128/512/2048 tokens. Gate lines at 70% and 85%. llama.cpp was not run on Qwen2.5-1.5B.

| model | engine | 128 | 512 | 2048 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SmolLM2-135M | vLLM | 44.5% | 46.8% | 42.3% |
| SmolLM2-135M | llama.cpp F16 + flash-attn | 44.0% | 43.1% | 40.2% |
| SmolLM2-135M | ours (CUDA graph) | 30.1% | 29.8% | 27.5% |
| Qwen2.5-1.5B | vLLM | 93.8% | 95.0% | 93.4% |
| Qwen2.5-1.5B | ours (CUDA graph) | 81.1% | 75.8% | 62.7% |

Percentages use the 263.5 GB/s sustained read denominator (see the README overclock note).

## Figure 2 — context scaling before and after the split-K fix

`results/fig2_context_scaling.png` / `.pdf`

| prompt | before (ms/token) | after (ms/token) |
|---|---|---|
| 128 | 4.80 | 3.39 |
| 512 | 4.30 | 3.43 |
| 2048 | 6.69 | 3.72 |

Also plotted: vLLM (2.30 / 2.18 / 2.41 ms) and llama.cpp F16 + flash-attn (2.32 / 2.37 / 2.54 ms), plus the 1.02 ms weight-read floor.

The pre-fix 128-token point (4.80 ms) was measured before the steady-clock burn; the 512→2048 growth is the clean pre-fix signal.
